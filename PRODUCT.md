# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential freelance clients looking for high-quality software engineering and full-stack development expertise. Visitors need to quickly assess technical skills, view recent projects, read technical articles, and establish a reliable contact channel.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Divinne Amunega's engineering capabilities, professional experience, and design sensibilities. It serves as a premium digital home that builds immediate trust, demonstrates expert execution, and facilitates career and contract opportunities.

## Brand Personality
Luxury/Refined Minimalist. The experience feels editorial, precise, and understated. Confidence is shown through high typographic contrast, generous whitespace, smooth transitions, and flawless details rather than flashy decorations.

## Anti-references
- Saturated AI SaaS templates (cyan/purple gradients, generic dark mode with neon borders, floating grid overlays).
- Overly cluttered, text-heavy portfolios with no hierarchy.
- Flat, dull gray-on-gray developer templates that feel cold and templated.
- Side-stripe cards or excessive uppercase eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Restraint**: Prioritize space and typography. Let the content breathe and use size and weight contrast rather than visual noise.
- **Flawless Motion**: Animations (using GSAP and Lenis) must feel organic and serve to guide focus, never to distract or slow down the reader.
- **Perceptual Harmony**: Colors are clean, warm, and highly readable, leveraging precise OKLCH ratios to maintain strict accessibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for body and placeholder texts).
- Native support for reduced motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`) across all GSAP animations.
- Accessible focus states for keyboard navigation.
